# Contributor Covenant — Summary

## Our pledge
Everyone contributing to this educational security toolkit agrees to keep
discussions professional and inclusive, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism; focus on what is best for learners.
- Assume competence and good intent; attacks on LLMs in code, never on people.
- All security research discussed must be within authorized scopes.

## Enforcement
Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers listed in CODEOWNERS.
Maintainers may remove comments, issues, or PRs that violate this covenant.

Adapted from Contributor Covenant v2.1 (contributor-covenant.org).
